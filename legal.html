Privacy Policy

IslaKeys collects only the details needed to match you with a licensed broker: your name, email address, mobile number, and the property preferences you choose to share.

  • We use your information to respond to inquiries and to connect you with PRC-licensed brokers.
  • We do not sell your personal data. Brokers see only the inquiries you send them.
  • We handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@islakeys.ph.
